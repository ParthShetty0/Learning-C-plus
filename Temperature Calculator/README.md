readme_content = """# Temperature Converter (Celsius & Fahrenheit)

A simple, lightweight utility to convert temperatures between Celsius (°C) and Fahrenheit (°F).

---

## 📐 Formulas

- **Celsius to Fahrenheit:**  
  `°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32`

- **Fahrenheit to Celsius:**  
  `°C = (°F - 32) × 5/9`
